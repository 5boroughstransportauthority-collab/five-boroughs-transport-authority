Five Boroughs Transportation Authority LLC was founded to provide high-quality transportation services to individuals in need.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable, and respectful non-emergency medical transportation while building a sustainable company that values both its passengers and its workforce. We operate according to three guiding principles:
Safety, Reliability, and Professionalism We focus on providing consistent, well-coordinated transportation that meets non-emergency medical needs. Every trip is approached with care, patience, and attention to detail.

Fair Treatment and Sustainable Employment We believe quality service starts with a supported workforce. As we grow, we are committed to fair compensation, clear expectations, and opportunities for long-term development aligned with the company’s stability.

Our vision is to build a dependable, community-focused non-emergency medical transportation provider known for consistency, professionalism, and trust. By focusing on steady growth and accountable operations, Five Borough Transport Authority LLC aims to become a long-term partner for patients, providers, and the communities we serve.

Our Story

Five Boroughs Transport Authority LLC was built from experience—not assumptions.

With nearly two decades of exposure to the accessory and transit industries, our team has observed how transportation systems operate in real-world conditions. We’ve seen where processes break down, where communication fails, and how those issues affect both passengers and the people responsible for transporting them.

Starting this company meant taking a measured and responsible approach. Rather than making promises we couldn’t stand behind, we focused on building a foundation rooted in compliance, preparation, and realistic growth. Five Boroughs Transport Authority LLC was created to provide dependable non-emergency medical transportation while steadily raising standards through consistency and accountability.

Our Unique Methods

We also have certified wheelchair securement systems in order to accomodate our clients. Our regular safety audits exceeds state requirements and creates an even better option for a smoother ride.
