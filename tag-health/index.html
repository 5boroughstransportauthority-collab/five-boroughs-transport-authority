Job Title: Dispatcher

Job Summary:

We are seeking highly organized and communicative Dispatchers to coordinate the movement
of our ambulette drivers, ensure timely pickups and drop-offs, and provide exceptional customer
service. As a mediator between customers and employees, you will play a critical role in
resolving issues and ensuring seamless operations.

Key Responsibilities:

1. Coordinate Driver Schedules: Monitor driver availability, assign trips, and adjust schedules as
needed to ensure efficient operations.
2. Dispatch Drivers: Communicate trip details to drivers, provide real-time updates, and ensure
drivers are aware of any changes or issues.
3. Customer Service: Respond to customer inquiries, resolve issues, and provide timely updates
on trip status.
4. Troubleshoot Issues: Handle driver-related issues, such as accidents, traffic delays, or
vehicle breakdowns, and communicate with customers and drivers to find solutions.
5. Maintain Accurate Records: Keep detailed records of trips, driver schedules, and customer
interactions.
6. Communicate with Drivers: Provide drivers with important information, such as traffic updates,
road closures, or changes in trip schedules.
7. Mediate Conflicts: Resolve conflicts between customers and drivers, and escalate issues to
management as needed.
8. Monitor Performance Metrics: Track key performance indicators (KPIs), such as on-time
performance, trip completion rates, and customer satisfaction.

Requirements:

1. High School Diploma: Required
2. Previous Dispatch Experience: Preferred
3. Excellent Communication Skills: Verbal and written communication skills are essential for this role.
4. Problem-Solving Abilities: Must be able to think critically and resolve issues quickly
5. Organizational Skills: Ability to multitask, prioritize tasks, and maintain accurate records
6. Technical Skills: Proficient in dispatch software, GPS tracking systems, and other relevant technologies.
7. Customer Service Skills: Must be able to provide exceptional customer service and resolve
conflicts in a professional manner.
