Job Title: Vehicle Operator - Ambulette

Job Summary:

We are seeking a reliable and safety-conscious Vehicle Operator to transport patients safely
and efficiently in our ambulette. The ideal candidate will have excellent driving skills, a strong
focus on customer service, and the ability to work well under pressure.
Responsibilities:
- Safely transport patients to and from medical appointments, hospitals, and other healthcare
facilities
- Assist patients with entering and exiting the ambulette, if needed
- Ensure the ambulette is clean, well-maintained, and stocked with necessary supplies
- Maintain accurate records of trips, including patient information and trip details
- Provide excellent customer service to patients, families, and healthcare providers
- Work collaboratively with dispatchers, drivers, and other staff members to ensure efficient
operations
- Comply with all company policies, procedures, and safety protocols
- Participate in ongoing training and professional development to maintain required certifications
and improve skills

Requirements:

- High school diploma or equivalent required
- Valid driver's license with a clean driving record
- Commercial Driver's License (CDL) or ability to obtain within [timeframe]
- Basic Life Support (BLS) certification or ability to obtain within [timeframe]
- Ability to lift, push, and pull patients and equipment as needed
- Excellent communication and customer service skills
- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and adapt to changing situations
- Reliable, punctual, and able to maintain a high level of professionalism
Preferred Qualifications:
- Previous experience as a vehicle operator or driver in a healthcare setting
- Knowledge of local roads and traffic patterns
- Ability to speak multiple languages

How to Apply:

If you are a motivated and compassionate individual who is committed to providing excellent
patient care, please submit your application, including your resume and a cover letter, to
Info@fiveboroughstransportauthority.com.
