About Our Services

Five Boroughs Transport Authority LLC provides medical transportation services, including ambulette transportation for patients requiring assisted mobility, scheduled transportation for individuals with special needs, and point-to-point transportation for medical appointments and essential services. Each trip is handled with professionalism, care, and respect.
